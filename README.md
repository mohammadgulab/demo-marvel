# Marvel Universe, a fan tribute

A fan-made cinematic scroll demo. Seven heroes appear one after another, each in its own pinned scene, with a face reveal on Iron Man and Spider-Man. All artwork is original fan art drawn in SVG and canvas.

Fan-made tribute demo. Not affiliated with or endorsed by Marvel or Disney. All characters belong to their owners.
